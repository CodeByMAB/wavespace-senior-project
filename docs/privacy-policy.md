# Wavespace Bitcoin Wallet — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 10, 2026  
**App:** Wavespace Bitcoin Wallet (self-custodial Bitcoin wallet)

This Privacy Policy describes how the Wavespace Bitcoin Wallet mobile application (“**App**”) handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

## 1. No Wavespace-operated data collection

Wavespace does **not** operate backend servers that collect, store, or process your personal data for analytics, advertising, or account management in connection with this App.

- We do **not** send your wallet balances, transaction history, contacts, or seed phrase to Wavespace-controlled servers.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the App.
- We do **not** track you across apps or websites for marketing purposes.

Wallet data needed to use Bitcoin (for example, keys and payment state managed by the wallet software on your device) remains **on your device** or is handled according to the third-party wallet infrastructure described below.

## 2. Self-custodial wallet

The App is **self-custodial**. You control your recovery phrase (seed). You are responsible for backing it up securely. If you lose your recovery phrase, we cannot recover your funds.

## 3. Breez SDK and Lightning / Bitcoin infrastructure

The App uses the **Breez SDK** (third-party software and related services) to provide Lightning Network and related Bitcoin functionality. When you use the App, certain technical operations (for example, connecting to the Lightning/Bitcoin network, syncing, or routing payments) may involve Breez and other network participants as required for those features to work.

For how Breez handles data, please review the **Breez privacy policy**:  
[https://breez.technology/privacy/](https://breez.technology/privacy/)  
(If the URL changes, refer to the privacy information published on Breez’s official website.)

Wavespace does not control Breez’s infrastructure; use of those features is also subject to Breez’s terms and policies.

## 4. Camera permission

The App may request access to your device **camera** only to **scan QR codes** (for example, payment or connection details). Images are processed for decoding; the App does **not** use the camera to photograph or store arbitrary gallery content for Wavespace.

## 5. Biometric authentication

If you enable biometric unlock (for example, Face ID or fingerprint), **biometric data is processed only on your device by the operating system**. Wavespace does not receive or store your biometric templates.

## 6. Device storage and OS services

The App may use standard on-device storage and operating system services (for example, secure storage APIs) to save preferences and wallet-related data locally. This processing occurs on your device under your OS vendor’s privacy terms.

## 7. Communications with you

If you contact support, we will use the information you choose to provide (for example, email address and message content) only to respond to your request.

## 8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
